# TAL Company site

Before changing copy or design, read:

- `PRODUCT.md`: the telos, the idea held whole, the page's levels and
  readers, and the messaging rules.
- `DESIGN.md`: the design system, the type roles, the copy budgets, and what
  has already failed.

## How the work is done

- Start from the telos and from what the reader sees on each screen, never
  from the content you have.
- Write and design copy as one act: each element's job, place, type role and
  word budget come first, and the sentence is written to fit them.
- Rewrite copy whole from the through-line; never patch it note by note.
- No example, demo, pasted source line or provenance carries the argument.
- After every change: `just measure`, look at screenshots at 1440, 1280 and
  390, then read the whole page top to bottom as a stranger.
- Judge the result, never the steps taken to reach it.

## Skills and reviews

- `write-the-page`: the method for writing or rewriting any sheet.
- `remove-claudeish`: on every piece of copy, before it is shown.
- `measure-outcome`, then `govern-next`: after each task, before the next.
- `.agents/agents/editor.md`: the editor's review of the page against the
  telos, when a version is ready.

## Shipping

Evolve the existing design; never restart it. Commit everything in the
working tree. Push to `preview` only after `just check` and `just measure`
pass; `just ship` promotes to production.
