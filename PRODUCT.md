# TAL product and positioning

This file is the record the page is built from. The page is checked against
it, never the other way round.

## What TAL is

TAL builds organizations the way they will now have to run: whole, explicit,
and executable, from the authority at the top to the code underneath. TAL
consults and builds products; the products are the problems met in every
organization, solved once and given away.

## The conviction

An organization can now be made to run on its own. Work runs on judgment no
rule ever caught; that judgment is knowledge the organization already has;
it can be made explicit; and an AI agent can act on it across a whole stream
of work, safely, because the world it acts in is built so the wrong outcome
cannot exist.

## The page

The page exists to change a serious, skeptical reader: they leave believing
an organization can now be made to run on its own, because the work in front
of them proves it, and they read the book, build with the tools, or write.

The site is an imprint, not a firm. It states one thesis and the three steps
that carry a reader to it, and each step is proven by a work published under
TAL's name. The reader meets the work itself on the page, in its own words,
never a description of what it proves.

1. **Title page.** The imprint small, as a colophon; the thesis as the
   largest type; under it the contents: each step, numbered, with the work
   that proves it, linking to its sheet.
2. **01, The Automated Organization.** "The judgment that keeps an
   organization moving is knowledge it already has." The book's own line on
   agents being given the manual, and its five-question test for tracing a
   case, which a reader can run on their own organization.
3. **02, ONTOK.** "That knowledge can be made explicit, as the
   organization's own kinds and proven facts." Each answer the test gives,
   matched to an ONTOK kind; the core's fourteen kinds; a fact exists only
   once proven.
4. **03, Type Construction Architecture.** "An agent can act on it alone and
   safely, because the wrong outcome cannot exist." The five failures the
   repo's worked example makes impossible, and the agent working through
   the same types, with nothing for an approver to catch.
5. **Author.** Portrait, one sentence on what he works on, and
   correspondence: the address, plain, never a button.

Each sheet carries its work's real image and its entry: kind, status and
year, the title as the link, one line. The step and its evidence live in the
work's content file.

No method, no cycle, no drawn explainers, no code, no call-to-action button.

### Where the proof is still owed

- No public piece yet shows a real stream of an organization's work run end
  to end by an agent on ONTOK kinds and TCA types, exceptions included. Sheet
  03 proves the wrong outcome cannot exist for a program, not yet for a
  stream. A published reference stream is what would carry the thesis whole.
- The book's chapters arguing steps 02 and 03 are not written yet.

## Voice

- Declare, plainly. No hedging, no hype.
- "We" and "our": TAL speaks as a company.
- Never describe TAL or the founder with adjectives about quality, rarity,
  or expertise. Never the founder's résumé.
- No selling. Contact is correspondence: the address, plain, under the
  author. Nothing on the page asks.
- The remove-claudeish skill (`.agents/skills/remove-claudeish/SKILL.md`)
  is run on every copy change.
